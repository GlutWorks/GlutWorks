A. The body is the menu

The screen is your own figure, turning slowly. Every slot hangs off the part it belongs to, and choosing a part shows what is stored there. There is no item list anywhere.

B. The list, with the body as a locator

The closest to a Fallout device: tabs and one sortable list you drive with the keyboard. The figure on the right lights up where the selected thing sits, next to a scan of its own model.

C. Laid out by where it is carried

One column per place, each with its own slots and weight limit. Big things take two slots. Whatever you have opened in the world, a crate, a body, a cupboard, joins as the last column, and things are dragged across.

D. The same figure when you search someone

Not a fourth layout, a use of the figure that any of the three gets for free. A search goes part by part and takes time, so what is hidden deep is found last or not at all.
